### **1\. Meeting Objective / Purpose**

To finalize the structural corrosion treatment strategy for the Uppercamp project before office construction commences, and to advance the conceptual design of the new roof support system to allow for architectural, HVAC, and electrical coordination.

### **2\. Executive Summary**

The project faces urgent scheduling constraints regarding the structural corrosion treatment of the Uppercamp walls. To avoid clashes with upcoming office construction, the team is aggressively sourcing quotes from alternative vendors (Zipex, Spray Lock) following an unsatisfactory initial quote from Sovocore. The team agreed to sequence the work so that demolition occurs before the corrosion treatment, ensuring dust does not compromise the treatment's bonding. Concurrently, the team is finalizing the conceptual design for the roof, adopting a Bond Deck system and steel frame structure, with a live load capacity of 3 kN/sqm. Preliminary designs and structural weights will be sent to the architect and Quantity Surveyor (Harvey) immediately to validate spatial coordination and budget feasibility before proceeding to detailed design.

### **3\. Agenda Items & Discussion Notes**

* **Corrosion Treatment Urgency**: Camilo emphasized the need for a finalized treatment methodology. Demolition of non-structural elements starts in roughly two weeks (pending city approval).  
* **Vendor Status**: Daniel reported Sovocore's quote was unsatisfactory (proposed more investigations rather than quoting based on existing quantities). Daniel pushed back and is waiting for a revision.  
* **Alternative Solutions**: Daniel is meeting with Sasa (Zipex) for a site walk-through tomorrow to obtain an alternative quote. Camilo also suggested investigating an American product called "Spray Lock," which acts as a concrete sealant and can be painted over.  
* **Sequencing**: The team agreed that corrosion treatment should happen *after* the non-structural demolition to ensure dust does not impede the bonding of surface treatments to the concrete.  
* **Roof Concept Design**: Daniel is preparing preliminary sketches for the new roof support system, to be delivered to Nona (architect) by Wednesday for ceiling, HVAC, and electrical coordination.  
* **Roof Construction Methodology**: The team discussed using a Bond Deck system with IBR sheeting. They are exploring cellular or I-section beams to accommodate ducting and achieve an industrial aesthetic without relying on internal columns.  
* **Budget Constraints**: Camilo instructed the team to provide the concept design and quantities (steel weights, surface areas) to Harvey (QS) immediately to ensure financial viability and secure client (Joel) approval prior to entering the detailed design phase.  
* **Roof Loading**: The roof is designed as an accessible roof with a live load of 3 kN/m². The team needs to confirm with the client regarding concentrated loads from HVAC or heavy planters.

## **Decisions Made**

* **Decision 1**: Demolition will precede the corrosion protection treatment to ensure proper surface bonding and minimize dust interference.  
* **Decision 2**: The structural loading requirement for the new accessible roof is established at 3 kN per square meter.  
* **Decision 3**: The conceptual roof design will proceed using a Bond Deck system over a steel frame (web angles and I-sections), avoiding internal columns where possible.
